3 · How should it choose?

Two ways to run. The difference is who decides which mechanisms get tried.

Autonomous
A bandit picks mechanisms, learns from what worked on this protein, and keeps a running leaderboard. Use this when you don't know what's wrong.
Guided
You choose the mechanisms from the library; only those are applied. Use this when you have a hypothesis — "this thing aggregates, fix the surface".
